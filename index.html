Good to know
A rough guide for a gathering: 300–400g per person for a braai (more if it's the main event), about 250g per person for a stew or seswaa, and roughly one chicken for every 3–4 people. Tell us your headcount in the cutting notes and we'll suggest a packing list.
Yes Halaal slaughter is available on request. Just note it in your order or mention it on WhatsApp and we'll arrange it for your beef or chicken.
Everything goes home vacuum-packed and labelled. Keep it in the fridge if you'll cook within a few days, otherwise freeze it the same day. Vacuum-packed beef freezes well for several months; portion it the way you'll use it so you only defrost what you need.
Absolutely. Choose a quarter, half or whole carcass from the beef list. We hang and age it, then cut it to your packing list steaks, roasts, stewing, mince and bones, all labelled. It's the best value per kilo and a popular choice for families and functions.
Whatever's easiest: cash on collection or delivery, mobile money (Orange Money / MyZaka), or EFT. We'll send the exact amount and details on WhatsApp once your order is weighed.
